## 6. Risks

- **Not financial, tax or legal advice.** Check tax, VAT and record-keeping rules with a qualified accountant.
- **Pressure on customers.** Reminder messages must be polite and accurate. Do not send them to people who have already paid or disputed an invoice.
- **Money data.** The sheet shows your finances. Keep it private, backed up, and out of shared chats.
- **Not for you if** you have no regular customers yet. Find the first sales before building the invoicing routine.
- **No earnings promise.** This kit helps you see and collect the money you have earned. It does not promise more of it.
