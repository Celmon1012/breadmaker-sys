## 6. Risks

- **Prices.** A price set from your costs can still be wrong for your market. Test a change on a few customers before you apply it to everyone.
- **Turning customers away.** Rules about who you will not take on must be fair and applied the same way to everyone. Base them only on fit, behaviour and profit.
- **Not for you if** you are still finding what customers want. Settle the offer by selling first, and write the sheet once you have sales to learn from.
- **No earnings promise.** This kit helps you state and price one offer clearly. It does not promise higher revenue.
