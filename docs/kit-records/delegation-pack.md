## 6. Risks

- **Quality can drop** while someone learns. Keep a check on the first few jobs, and tell the person what you are checking.
- **Authority.** If you give someone a decision, accept their call inside the limits you wrote. Overriding them silently teaches them to wait for you.
- **People and employment.** Handing work to staff or contractors has legal and contractual implications. Check your obligations before you change anyone's duties.
- **Not for you if** you work alone with no one to hand work to. Start with the checklist kit and come back to this once you have help.
- **No earnings promise.** This kit reduces how much depends on you. It does not promise growth or free time.
