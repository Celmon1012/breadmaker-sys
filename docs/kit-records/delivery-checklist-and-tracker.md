## 6. Risks

- **A checklist can go stale.** If the way you work changes and the checklist does not, it becomes wrong
  and people stop trusting it. Put a review date on it.
- **Customer data.** The tracker holds names, addresses and order details. Keep it in one private place
  with limited access and a backup.
- **People.** A checklist does not replace training. Whoever runs a job from it for the first time needs
  you reachable.
- **Not for you if** your work is different every time. A checklist fits repeatable jobs, and one-off
  projects need a different kind of plan.
- **No earnings promise.** This kit makes delivery repeatable. It does not promise more sales or time off.
