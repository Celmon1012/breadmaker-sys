## 6. Risks

- **Messaging rules.** Follow-ups sent on WhatsApp or email must go to people who contacted you or agreed to
  hear from you. Do not message cold lists. Check the rules that apply in your country.
- **Customer data.** The enquiry sheet holds names and contact details. Keep it in one private place with a
  backup, and record consent where you collect it.
- **Not for you if** you get fewer than a handful of enquiries a month: the sheet adds effort before it
  pays back, and the first problem to solve is lead flow, not follow-up.
- **No earnings promise.** This kit helps you stop losing enquiries. It does not promise more sales.
