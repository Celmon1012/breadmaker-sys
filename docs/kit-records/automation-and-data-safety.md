## 6. Risks

- **Automations act without you.** Start with low-stakes tasks, keep a log of what they send, and have a way to stop them. Never automate a message you would not want a customer to read twice.
- **Customer data and the law.** In Nigeria this includes the NDPA. Collect only what you need, record consent, and have a plan if data leaks. This is not legal advice.
- **Backups and keys.** A backup you have not restored is a hope. Keep passwords and keys out of shared documents and chats, and rotate anything that was ever shared.
- **Not for you if** you have no repeating tasks yet. Run the business by hand until the same task shows up three times.
- **No earnings promise.** This kit saves time and protects data. It does not promise more revenue.
