# Homepage video script: "How Breadmaker works"

**Length:** about 2 minutes 20 seconds at a calm speaking pace (roughly 330 spoken words).
**Where it goes:** the homepage video slot. Host the file (YouTube, Vimeo or Loom, unlisted is fine) and set `VITE_VSL_URL` in the Coolify build variables.
**Format:** you on camera for the opening and close, screen recordings in between. One take per section is fine; do not over-edit.
**Rules:** no income claims, no promises of results, no "guaranteed". Say what we do and what you get, not what you will earn.

Words in [brackets] are on-screen directions, not spoken.

---

## 1. Hook (0:00 to 0:15)

[On camera, plain background]

"If a family or a business depends on you, you do not need more ideas. Every week there is a new way to earn: a funnel, a bot, an agent, a course. Most of them are untested, and you cannot afford to be the test."

[Text on screen: "You do not need more ideas. You need what works."]

## 2. The idea (0:15 to 0:35)

[Cut to the homepage headline]

"I'm [NAME], from Breadmaker Systems. A breadwinner is the person others lean on. A breadmaker is the system they use to carry that weight. We find, test and teach those systems. If it has not been tested and proven, it is not listed."

## 3. Find the leak first (0:35 to 1:05)

[Screen recording: the Leak Test, answering a few questions, then the score and the biggest leak]

"Most small businesses do not have a sales problem. They have a leak. A slow reply. An offer nobody can repeat back. Work that only happens when you are in the room.

So we start with a free test. Thirty questions, about four minutes. It scores six places a business leaks: offer, sales, operations, cash, team and technology. And it tells you the one to fix first."

[Text on screen: "Offer · Sales · Operations · Cash · Team · Tech"]

## 4. One system for each leak (1:05 to 1:35)

[Screen recording: the Systems page, scrolling the six cards, then opening one kit's sessions]

"For each leak there is a system. A kit with the steps in order: what to do this week, one action to finish, and a clear line for when it is done. You finish with something running, not something bookmarked.

If you would rather not do it alone, our programs build several of those systems with you. Launchpad gets a working page live in seven days. Foundry builds the whole system in thirty, on accounts and code you own."

## 5. Why you can trust it (1:35 to 1:55)

[On camera, closer]

"Here is our rule. We run every system ourselves first. We write down what we set up, what happened and what went wrong. And we say what could go wrong on the page. We promise a working system, not a sales number."

[Text on screen: "Run it first · Write it down · Name the risk"]

## 6. Next step (1:55 to 2:20)

[On camera, then the Leak Test landing page]

"So start with the test. It is free, it takes about four minutes, and it tells you where to begin. The link is right below this video. If you want to see what we build first, the systems are on this page.

Breadwinners provide. We build the systems they use."

[End card: "Take the free Business Leak Test" with the link]

## Recording notes

- **Camera:** phone is fine. Landscape, natural light facing a window, mic within an arm's length. Record the screen sections separately with your normal screen recorder.
- **Delivery:** speak slower than feels natural. Short pauses at each section break give you clean cut points.
- **Captions:** add them. Most people watch with the sound off, and it helps people who read English as a second language.
- **Thumbnail:** your face and the line "Where is your business leaking?".
- **Placeholders:** `[NAME]` and the exact Leak Test timing ("about four minutes") match what the site says (about four minutes), so time the test yourself before you record.
- **Before you publish:** check every line against `docs/POSITIONING.md`'s honesty rule. If you change the script, keep the "no earnings promise" line and do not add any figures you cannot show a record for.
